The idea behind the work

Verdentra is a cloud, data and AI consultancy working with software vendors. My job is to make the company easy to understand, easy to trust and a place people want to work.

I own the social media output, and I take every campaign from the first idea through to creation and publishing. I also run internal communications and engagement activities, so the way Verdentra talks to its own team matches the way it talks to the world. The work below includes Interns, Unfiltered, a series built on interns’ own words, recruitment calls for the blog team, welcome templates for new joiners, and celebration pieces such as the third anniversary wallpaper and the annual office trip.

Outside the channels, I lead Verdentra Waves, the company’s CSR programme, planning and running the events that put its values into practice.

How thiscame together

  1. 01

    Understand

    Get clear on the audience, whether that is a prospective client, a candidate or the team, and on what the company needs them to feel.

  2. 02

    Ideate

    Turn that into campaign ideas, content pillars and creative directions before anything is designed.

  3. 03

    Create

    Produce the posts, carousels, graphics and event materials myself, so the idea and the finished piece stay connected.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Publish on a steady calendar, run the events, then look at what worked and adjust the next round.
